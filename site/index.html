Common questions

What does 99.9% uptime mean?

At most about 43 minutes of downtime a month. It’s a common target for business software, and it leaves enough time for a person to be alerted and fix a problem.

Do you cover nights and weekends?

Not yet. Monitoring runs 24/7 and alerts go to your team, with runbooks so whoever is on call knows what to do. I take over during business hours.

Do we have to be on Azure?

No. Azure and .NET are my specialty, but monitoring, alerting, safe deploys, backups and incident response work the same way on any cloud.

What access do you need?

For the audit, read-only access to your cloud console, monitoring and code repositories. I’m happy to sign your NDA first.

Why not just hire an SRE?

You may want to eventually. A full-time hire costs more than any of these plans and takes months to find. Many teams start here, and I can help write the job description and interview when you’re ready.

Can we cancel?

Yes. Monthly plans are month to month with 30 days’ notice. The audit is a one-time fixed fee.

Why “Third Nine”?

99.9% uptime is called “three nines.” The third nine is the hard one to earn, and it’s the one I help you keep.
